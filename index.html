01 · Our Reading of RFP:2026-026

We've read the brief — carefully.

Migrating off Odoo isn't a templated job. Career Lab is a training and qualifications brand — NCFE, CACHE, special-needs programmes — with audiences that judge credibility from the first page they land on. Here's what we believe matters most.

01.A

A clean break from Odoo

Odoo's website module locks layout, structure and content into proprietary modules. A clean export, content audit, and disciplined URL mapping protects every drop of existing SEO equity you've earned at careerlab.ae.

01.B

Programmes as the hero

Qualifications — NCFE, CACHE, Special Needs — deserve more than bullet points. We'll structure each programme as a discoverable, schema-marked landing page with eligibility, enrolment, and a conversion-focused CTA.

01.C

Events that actually convert

Most training-website event modules are afterthoughts. We'll build event listings with registration flows that capture qualified leads — not just a date and a Google form.

01.D

A CMS your team will actually use

The brief is explicit: the CMS must be friendly to non-technical staff. WordPress with a clean block editor, custom field groups for programmes and events, and recorded training — not a system that needs a developer for every change.

01.E

Performance, security, scale

Mobile-responsive, Core Web Vitals optimised, SSL, daily backups, malware monitoring, and a hosting stack that won't buckle when a TV mention or LinkedIn post sends a traffic spike.

01.F

One month is tight — we'll be honest

A realistic 4-week timeline is achievable for the Starter and Professional tiers, provided content sign-off and brand assets land on time. The Premium build benefits from 5–6 weeks to do justice to the fully custom UI/UX work.

02 · Live Audit of careerlab.ae

We audited the current site. Here's what we found.

Before pricing the rebuild, we ran a diagnostic of the live website. Eight findings worth bringing to your attention — not as criticism, but as concrete evidence that migration off Odoo is the right decision.

Critical
Warning
Opportunity
Critical
FINDING 01

Your CMS name is leaking into Google search results

One of your indexed pages appears in Google as "Odoo Courses Homepage | Career Lab". The platform is literally announcing itself in the page title — a default Odoo template string that was never overridden.

Why it matters: Visitors searching for your programmes see a generic CMS name where your brand should be. It quietly damages credibility before they've even clicked the result.
Critical
FINDING 02

Your admin login page is publicly indexed

The Odoo backend login is indexed in Google at careerlab.ae/web/login — discoverable to anyone searching. It even inherits the homepage meta description.

Why it matters: Beyond looking unprofessional in search results, indexed admin URLs are a known soft target for credential-stuffing and brute-force attempts.
Critical
FINDING 03

Your strongest USP is buried on a sub-page

"Career Lab is the only NCFE-approved center in Abu Dhabi" — that's a category-defining advantage. Right now it appears halfway down the /cache page, not on the homepage hero where it belongs.

Why it matters: First-time visitors leave within 8 seconds without finding a reason to stay. Your single most powerful differentiator should be the first sentence anyone reads.
Warning
FINDING 04

Platform-specific URLs in production

Your courses page lives at /slides — Odoo's internal name for its e-learning module. Not memorable, not user-friendly, and immediately telegraphs the underlying platform.

Why it matters: URL slugs are an SEO ranking factor. A page at /programs/cache-level-3-early-years outperforms /slides in both rankings and click-through rate.
Warning
FINDING 05

Duplicate, generic meta descriptions

The homepage, login page, and several other URLs share the same generic "Early Childhood Education Programs…" description. Search engines treat duplicate meta as a low-quality signal.

Why it matters: Each page needs a unique, keyword-relevant description that earns the click. Duplicate descriptions cost you snippet space, organic CTR, and rich-result eligibility.
Warning
FINDING 06

Thin programme architecture

NCFE Level 2, Level 3, Level 4 SEND, and Level 5 are four distinct qualifications with different audiences, eligibility, and price points — yet they're currently compressed into a single /cache category page.

Why it matters: Google can only rank one page per qualification. Right now you're competing with yourself instead of owning four separate top-ranking results.
Opportunity
FINDING 07

No structured data — rich results being missed

The FAQ content on /cache is high quality and exactly what Google wants to surface as rich snippets. But there's no FAQ schema markup. Same story for Course, Event, and Educational Organization schemas — all absent.

Why it matters: Structured data is the single easiest SEO win available right now. It costs nothing to add and can double organic click-through rates on programme pages.
Opportunity
FINDING 08

No content engine for top-funnel traffic

The RFP lists Blog/Insights as optional. For a training brand competing in Abu Dhabi's early-years education market, it shouldn't be — it's the lowest-cost way to capture parents and educators searching for guidance before they're ready to enrol.

Why it matters: "How to become an early years educator in UAE" type searches are your future student funnel. Without published content, you're invisible at the discovery stage.
The honest takeaway: None of these issues are about Career Lab's content or programmes — those are strong. They're about platform limitations baked into Odoo's website module. A clean migration solves all eight findings in a single project.

PHASE 01
Days 1–4

Discovery

Kickoff workshop · brand asset review · content audit of existing Odoo site · sitemap & wireframe sign-off · technical hosting decisions.

PHASE 02
Days 5–11

Design

UI/UX mockups for homepage and key internals · brand alignment · client review rounds · final design sign-off before development.

PHASE 03
Days 12–22

Development

WordPress build · custom layouts · CMS configuration · content migration from Odoo · feature integration (courses, events, forms) · SEO foundation.

PHASE 04
Days 23–27

Testing

Cross-browser QA · device testing (desktop, tablet, mobile) · performance & load testing · accessibility checks · bug fixes · final client UAT.

PHASE 05
Days 28–30

Launch

DNS cutover · 301 redirect deployment · search console verification · CMS training session · go-live monitoring · post-launch support begins.

Honest disclosure: A 4-week window is achievable for Starter and Professional with timely content sign-off from Career Lab. The Premium tier benefits from 5–6 weeks given the custom UI/UX scope, Arabic-ready architecture, and CRM integration. We'd rather extend by a week than ship work we're not proud of.
